## Connecting Observations, Models and AI
{: .major}

EarthMapps is a research group led by Stef Lhermitte at the [Department of Earth & Environmental Science](https://ees.kuleuven.be/en) of KU Leuven. We study how ecosystems, landscapes and the cryosphere respond to climate variability, environmental change and natural disturbances.

Our research combines satellite observations, climate and biophysical models, and emerging geospatial AI methods to better understand, monitor and predict processes across the Earth system. We develop innovative approaches for analysing large spatio-temporal datasets, building model emulators, and integrating observations with environmental models. These methods are applied to topics including ecosystem resilience, biodiversity, drought, glaciers, ice sheets, sea-level rise and climate extremes.

By bringing together Earth observation, environmental sciences, modelling and artificial intelligence, we aim to improve our understanding of a changing planet and contribute to more informed environmental decision making. Through research, teaching and open science, we contribute to advancing data-driven Earth and environmental science.
