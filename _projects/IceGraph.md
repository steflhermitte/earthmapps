### Project description

Antarctic mass loss remains a major source of uncertainty in sea-level-rise projections, partly because complex physical processes are computationally demanding to represent in full-scale ice-sheet simulations. IceGraph develops a MeshGraphNet-based emulator that approximates complex ice-sheet models on irregular meshes. The project also incorporates damage physics into the Kori-ULB ice-flow model and examines how damage affects Antarctic mass loss and uncertainty in sea-level-rise projections.
